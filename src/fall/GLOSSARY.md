# Fall

How a board closes after tiles leave. Survivors fall to the floor, then empty cells refill from the supply.

**Floor**:
The bottom row of the board.
_Avoid_: Ground, base

**Fall**:
The straight-down movement of the tiles that remain, toward the floor, in their existing order.
_Avoid_: Drop, gravity, cascade

**Refill**:
The draw of tiles from the supply into cells a fall left empty.
_Avoid_: Spawn, deal
